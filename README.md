## ✨ One key. Two superpowers.

Most voice tools only turn speech into text. VoxPilot listens for **intent**: it works out whether you're *writing* or *asking*, and does the right thing.

| You say… | VoxPilot… |
| --- | --- |
| *"Hi Sarah, thanks for the update, I'll send it Friday — actually Thursday"* | ✍️ types **"Hi Sarah, thanks for the update. I'll send it Thursday."** where your cursor is |
| *"Open Notepad and write a shopping list"* | 🚀 opens Notepad and writes the list |
| *"Reply to this email saying I'll be 10 minutes late"* | 📧 writes the reply in the email you're looking at |
| *"Search YouTube for lo-fi music"* | 🔎 opens the search |
| *"Make this more formal"* (with text selected) | 🪄 rewrites the selection in place |
| *"What's 15% of 240?"* | 💬 answers out loud: *"36"* |

No commands to memorise. Just talk the way you'd talk to a colleague.

## 🧠 What makes it different

### ✍️ Writing that sounds like you, only cleaner
- **Fixes the "umm", "actually", "I mean":** it keeps what you *meant*, not every word you said.
- **Writing styles:** *Light* (your exact words, punctuated), *Polished* (grammar and spelling fixed) or *Elaborate* (notes turned into full sentences).
- **A style for each kind of app:** crisp for email, relaxed for chat (no stiff full stops), and complete for documents.
- **Spoken shortcuts:** say *"my address"* and your full address is typed.
- **Your words:** names, products and jargon are always spelled the way you want.

### 🚀 Actions in any app
- **Open anything you have installed** by name: desktop programs and Store apps alike.
- **Get things done inside apps and websites:** *"turn on dark mode in Settings"*, *"create a new GitHub repo called demo, make it private"*, *"fill this form with my name"*. VoxPilot reads what's on screen and works through it step by step.
- **Everyday controls:** switch windows, close tabs, save, undo, pause music, volume up.

### 🎛️ Modes that fit how you work
- **Smart:** types, answers or acts, whichever you meant.
- **Typing only:** pure dictation, nothing else.
- **Set per app**, e.g. *Typing only* in WhatsApp and *Smart* everywhere else.
- **Switch by voice:** *"switch to typing only"*, *"smart mode"*, *"typing only in this app"*.
- **Unsure? It plays safe.** If you're in a text box and a sentence *could* be an instruction, VoxPilot double-checks, then types it (or asks you, if you prefer).
- **Talking to VoxPilot directly?** Start with *"Hey VoxPilot…"* or *"Computer…"*.

### 🔁 Corrections by voice
Said it wrong? Say *"no, I meant…"* or *"undo that"*, and VoxPilot fixes what it just typed.

### 🗣️ It talks back
Answers and confirmations are spoken in a natural voice (Thalia, Luna, Apollo or Orion), or the Windows voice.

## 🔒 Private and safe by design

- **No audio kept:** recordings are deleted as soon as they're transcribed, and speech is sent with model training turned off.
- **History is off by default.** If you turn it on, it stays on your PC, and turning it off deletes it.
- **Keys encrypted** for your Windows account.
- **Asks before risky steps:** VoxPilot wants a spoken *yes* before risky buttons or opening a site you didn't name.
- **Off limits:** it never types into password boxes, password managers or Windows sign-in.
- **Screen text is data, never instructions,** so a web page can't take over your assistant.
- **Local audit log:** every action is recorded, never your words.
- **Usage and cost in plain sight:** minutes, characters and AI tokens by feature, with a cost estimate and an optional monthly budget.

## ⚡ Get started in 2 minutes

1. **[Download VoxPilot-Setup.exe](https://github.com/nivasthumma/voxpilot-releases/releases/latest/download/VoxPilot-Setup.exe)** and run it.
   > Windows may show *"Windows protected your PC"* while VoxPilot is new. Click **More info → Run anyway**.
2. **Add your keys** on the welcome screen. They're checked and encrypted on the spot:
   - 🎤 **Deepgram** for speech (enough for *Typing only*);
   - 🧠 **Claude** (Anthropic) **or Gemini** for *Smart* mode.
3. **Hold Ctrl + Win and say hello.** A short practice round walks you through dictation, hands-free and editing.

VoxPilot lives in the system tray. Click the microphone icon for **Home, History, Writing, Chat and Settings**.

## 🔄 Always up to date

VoxPilot checks this page for new versions and downloads them in the background. A download is used only if its **checksum matches** the published one. Click the tray notice to update in seconds, or it updates the next time VoxPilot starts. Your settings, keys and history always stay.

## ❓ FAQ

<details>
<summary><b>Which apps does it work in?</b></summary>

Any app where you can type: browsers, Outlook, Gmail, Word, Slack, Teams, WhatsApp, Notepad, code editors and more. Actions work across desktop apps and websites.
</details>

<details>
<summary><b>Do I need to install anything else?</b></summary>

No. Everything VoxPilot needs comes in the installer. It installs only for your Windows user and needs no admin rights.
</details>

<details>
<summary><b>What does it cost to run?</b></summary>

VoxPilot uses your own Deepgram and Claude or Gemini keys, so you pay those providers directly at their list prices. Speech recognition, for example, costs under half a cent per minute. **Settings → Usage** shows exactly what you've used. It picks a fast, inexpensive model for quick jobs and a stronger one only when a task needs it.
</details>

<details>
<summary><b>How do I uninstall it?</b></summary>

Go to **Settings → Apps → Installed apps → VoxPilot → Uninstall**. You choose whether to keep or delete your settings, keys and history.
</details>
